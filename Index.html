About Us

GreenPath Initiative for Sustainable Development is a community-based organization working to promote climate action, environmental protection, youth empowerment, and sustainable development in Cameroon and beyond.

Building a greener and more sustainable future.

Our Projects

☀️ Solar Lighting

Providing solar lighting solutions to support students and households with clean and reliable energy.

🌳 Tree Planting

Promoting tree planting and environmental education to help communities protect and restore nature.

♻️ Waste Management

Engaging young people in clean-up activities, waste sorting, recycling, and environmental awareness.

Our Impact

We engage young people and communities through practical environmental activities, climate education, tree planting, waste management, and clean energy initiatives.
